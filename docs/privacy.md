# Privacy and Safe Use

The repository's generated cohort and packaged P0001 demo are synthetic. The demo contains no known real patient records or direct identifiers. The service exposes the synthetic patient identifier and generated signals to the local demo UI.

This is a privacy-risk statement about the bundled demo, not a legal assessment or certification. It does not establish compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), HIPAA, or any other regulation. Do not enter real patient data, credentials, or protected health information. Before handling real data, obtain appropriate approvals, perform a privacy/security review, and define access, retention, consent, and incident-response controls.

The software is not validated for diagnosis, treatment selection, or clinical decision-making. All predictions and what-if outputs are simulations and must not be used as medical advice.
